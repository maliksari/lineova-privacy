Lineova Support

Lineova is a drawing, painting, vector and 3D texture painting studio for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: malik.sari06@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. Lineova works without sign-in.

Where are my drawings stored? On your device, or in your own iCloud Drive if you choose.

Which formats can I export? PNG, JPEG, HEIC, TIFF, WebP, PDF, APNG, SVG, layered PSD, OpenRaster, USDZ and GLB.
