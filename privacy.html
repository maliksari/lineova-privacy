Lineova Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Lineova does not collect personal data. There is no account or sign-in, and no analytics, tracking or advertising.

  • Your artwork stays on your device. If you enable iCloud, documents sync through your own iCloud account, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • Files leave the app only when you export or share them yourself.

Lineova Premium purchases

Lineova Premium is an optional weekly or yearly auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.

To check whether Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID and the App Store transaction information for your purchases. This purchase history is not linked to your identity, is used only to unlock Premium features, and is never used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Children: the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
