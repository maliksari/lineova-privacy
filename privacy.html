Lineova Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Lineova does not collect personal data such as your name, email address, contacts, location or photos. There is no account or sign-in, and no advertising or tracking across apps and websites.

  • Your artwork stays on your device. If you enable iCloud, documents sync through your own iCloud account, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • Files leave the app only when you export or share them yourself.

Lineova Premium purchases

Lineova is free to use. Lineova Premium is optional and is offered as a one-time Lifetime purchase (sometimes at a discounted special-offer price) and as a 3-Day Trial at no charge. These are not subscriptions and nothing renews. Payment is processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.

The app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to show the Lineova Premium screen and to check whether Premium is active. RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID;
  • the App Store transaction information for your Lineova purchases (purchase history);
  • how the Lineova Premium screen is used: when it is shown, which option is selected, whether a purchase is started and when the screen is closed, together with the app language and whether dark mode is on.

This data is not linked to your identity. It is used only to unlock Premium, restore your purchases and understand how well the Premium screen works. It is never used for tracking or advertising and is not sold. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion

Purchase records are kept for as long as they are needed to restore your purchases; Apple keeps its own record of every App Store transaction. Usage information about the Premium screen is kept by RevenueCat under its privacy policy.

Your artwork is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud account; deleting the app or the documents removes it. Because the app user ID is anonymous, we cannot find your data by name or email. To have your RevenueCat purchase record deleted, email us the order ID of your Lineova purchase from your Apple receipt (or from reportaproblem.apple.com), and we will delete it. If you delete it, restoring Premium later may require contacting us.

Earlier subscriptions

Earlier versions offered Premium as an auto-renewable subscription. If you still have one, you can manage or cancel it at any time in your App Store account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Children: the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
