Lineova Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Lineova does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Your artwork stays on your device. If you enable iCloud, documents sync through your own iCloud account, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • Files leave the app only when you export or share them yourself.

Children: the app collects no data from anyone, including children.
